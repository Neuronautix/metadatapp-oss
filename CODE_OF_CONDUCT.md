# Code of Conduct

Metadatapp contributors are expected to keep collaboration respectful, practical, and focused on the work.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful of different backgrounds, experience levels, and constraints.
- Discuss technical disagreements with evidence and concrete tradeoffs.
- Keep feedback specific, actionable, and relevant to the project.
- Assume public discussions may be read by future contributors.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Dismissive or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeated disruption of project discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments; close issues; block users; or take other reasonable action to protect contributors and users.

Report conduct issues privately by emailing `neuronautix@gmail.com`.
