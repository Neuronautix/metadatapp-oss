# Navigation

The left sidebar groups screens into sections. Which sections and items appear
depends on your **role** and the **feature flags** enabled for your instance, so
treat the lists below as the full catalogue rather than a guarantee.

## Research & Studies

The core ISA-style research records.

- **Investigations** — top-level research efforts.
- **Studies** — studies within an investigation.
- **Assays** — assays / procedures.
- **Datasets** — datasets attached to your research.
- **Import & Curation Hub** — orchestrates bulk import and curation.
- **Import Data (CSV)** — spreadsheet import (feature-gated).
- **Data Entry** — grid-based manual entry (feature-gated).
- **Data Curation** — the review-first curation workflow (feature-gated).
- **AI Assistant** — a governed, preview-only assistant that drafts metadata
  suggestions. See [AI Providers](ai-providers) for enabling it.

## Subjects & Bio-Resources

- **Subjects** — organism/subject records.
- **Samples** — samples / specimens.
- **Cages**, **Batches**, **Lines**, **Housing Management** — colony resources
  (feature-gated; some are specific to particular deployments).

## Operations & Environment

Facility and environmental monitoring (largely feature-gated, and tailored to
specific deployments): Calendar, Operations, Observatory, Facility Map, Sensors,
Conditions, Alarms, Rooms, Systems, and alerts.

## Data Intelligence & FAIR

FAIR tooling and analytics (feature-gated): Metadata Fusion, comparison and
analytics hubs, ATMP, the FAIR Navigator, **Metadata Catalog**, **FAIR
Export** (RO-Crate, FAIR²/JSON-LD), and the **HCM Metadata Form** — a read-only
viewer of the FAIR-aligned Home Cage Monitoring canonical form, grouped along the
ISA hierarchy (see [HCM Minimal Metadata Form](../metadata/hcm-minimal-metadata-form)).

## Connected Apps

- **App Directory** — the grid of available integrations; this is where you
  connect external systems. See [Connected Apps in the UI](connected-apps).
- **Live Showcase** — a demonstration view of integrations (feature-gated).

## System Administration

Mostly **admin-only**:

- **Users** and **Organizations** — tenant and account management.
- **Audit Log** — activity audit (admin-only, feature-gated).
- **API Keys** — server-side API key management.
- **AI Providers** — configure OpenAI/Anthropic keys. See [AI Providers](ai-providers).
- **Appearance** and **Profile** — available to all users.
- **Flag Studio** — toggle feature flags (admin-only).
- **Documentation** — links to this guide.
